and plugin ecosystems. There is currently **no single repository-wide license**
that overrides the licenses of included materials.

- A license distributed inside a skill or plugin directory applies to that

- Content without an explicit license remains subject to its original owner's

- Repository-level documentation and maintenance scripts require a license

`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` for provenance guidance.
